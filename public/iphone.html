Add it to your Home Screen

On the Serenity page in Safari:

  1. Tap Share in the toolbar (the square with an arrow, at the bottom of the screen).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Serenity’s icon appears on your Home Screen.
  4. Open Serenity from the icon. It runs full screen, like any app.

Good to know

  • Updates are automatic. You always get the newest version when you open it.
  • Keep the app open during a session. iPhones pause the audio if you lock the screen or switch apps.
  • Reminders work once it’s on your Home Screen and you allow notifications.
